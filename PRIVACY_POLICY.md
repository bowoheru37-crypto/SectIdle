# 🔒 PRIVACY POLICY — SECT IDLE & NUSA LEGENDS

**Last Updated: March 2026**

## 1. Overview
Sect Idle / Nusa Legends ("the Application") is committed to protecting user privacy. This Application is designed to operate primarily offline on mobile devices (including low-entry Android devices such as Itel A70) and retro console platforms.

## 2. Information Collection and Storage
- **Offline Save Data**: All gameplay progress, sect states, disciples, and UI layouts are stored locally on your device storage using JSON compression or SharedPreferences.
- **No Personal Tracking**: We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal identity data.
- **Gemini AI Integration (Optional)**: If you choose to enable dynamic dialogue or AI narrative generation, requests are sent via secure HTTPS API endpoints to Google Firebase / Gemini AI. No personal identify information is transmitted.

## 3. Data Protection
Your local save files and generated build export artifacts remain strictly on your local hardware unless you manually export or backup your save files.
